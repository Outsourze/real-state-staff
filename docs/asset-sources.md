## Visual substitutions

- The hero shows a closed laptop, books and large ocean-facing windows. It is a real photograph selected for the warm architectural composition; it is not a photograph of the client's office.
- The meeting room overlooks a snowy forest rather than a waterfront.
- The property photograph is a landscaped contemporary pool terrace rather than a New Zealand listing.
- The CTA photograph depicts Lake Matheson in New Zealand, as identified on its source page.
